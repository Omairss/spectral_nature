## Why the earlier working version did not need this on successful runs

The [actual accepted October 14 image comparison](historical-comparison.json) establishes the same Home schema, editor/application functions, format-repair routine and semantic reviewer. The relevant failure-before-review and missing-client control flow also matches. Whole agent/wrapper functions differ elsewhere in execution-budget resolution and source-access discovery; those differences are not evidence that the whole engine is identical.

The old implementation succeeded when synthesis returned a valid artifact. A missing required field exposed its latent recovery bug. The observed fresh failure was `items[12].cause_status` missing from a complete draft, not a new metadata requirement or a failure to collect research. Previously successful outputs do not establish that invalid model responses were recoverable. Source fidelity and successful imports missed that distinction.
