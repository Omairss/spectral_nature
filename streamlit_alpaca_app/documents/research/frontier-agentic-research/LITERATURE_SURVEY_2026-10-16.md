# Frontier literature survey and design assessment

As of **2026-10-16**. [Research TODOs and priorities](README.md) · [Ground truth and temporal evaluation](GROUND_TRUTH_2026-10-16.md)

**Owner constraint:** cheap open-source models are mandatory. This survey's proprietary-model results are external comparisons. The improvement program targets the [complete prediction machine](PREDICTION_MACHINE_SCOPE_2026-10-16.md); eligible cheaper/better models are [marked here](OPEN_MODEL_CANDIDATES_2026-10-16.md). Apply this constraint to any proposed teacher, grader or allocation experiment too.

## Findings

The relevant frontier is a **measured research system**: strong analysis with usable source/calculation tools, evidence-grounded evaluation, and selective allocation learned from trajectories. More agents, a more expensive model or a larger context structure are not independently sufficient.

We have several of the architectural ingredients. We have not demonstrated SOTA quality, and we cannot honestly give a percentage gap without a matched evaluation. The clearest current deficits are reliable derivation, critical source interpretation, review precision, timely correction, and evaluation/calibration. Our historical corpus is an opportunity for research, not evidence that these deficits have already been solved.

## Search and evidence standard

This is a targeted survey, not an exhaustive systematic review or an independent reproduction. Searches covered financial research/discovery evaluation, tool-agent harness optimization, verification/review, model routing, reasoning-intensive retrieval, agent memory, long context and temporal forecasting. Queries included the topic with `2026`, exact paper titles, `FrontierFinance`, `BigFinanceBench`, `FinResearchBench`, `agent harness`, `subtask routing`, and `agent memory`, followed by reference trails and official project pages. Search engines and third-party mentions supplied leads; the claims below use papers or official maintainer sources.

Twenty-six primary papers were screened. For the core finance/evaluation/optimization papers, inspection went beyond abstracts to methods, results/tables, cost definitions and limitations. For the adjacent context/memory/forecasting papers, inspection targeted relevant sections rather than every appendix. FinSearchComp, the April computer-use FrontierFinance, Sufficient Context and Lost in the Middle received scope/abstract screening only. The last two are close precedents also identified in the separate [publication assessment](../../architecture/overall/audits/PUBLICATION_PROSPECTS_2026-10-16.md). No experiment, paid model evaluation or repository implementation was run for this survey.

**SOTA is conditional on task, split, harness, tools, effort, evaluator version, budget and date.** An author's benchmark win is a reported result. A current official listing is a stronger dated comparison, still limited to listed submissions. Neither proves universal leadership or transfer to Spectral Nature. Public benchmark outcomes may also have been exposed during system development.

Earlier PAL, LLM-as-Judge and RouteLLM citations were foundational examples, not a current frontier survey. This document supersedes any implication otherwise.

Keep essential facts even when every compared system passes or fails them. |
| **[Hindsight Structured Advice Distillation (HSAD)](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.13000v1)** — October 9 preprint | An 8B advisor learns question-specific advice from graded alternative-strategy traces; the executor stays frozen. | Relevant candidate after existing-prompt/example baselines. | **[GEPA](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.19457v2)** — July 2025, revised February 14, 2026 | Reflective prompt evolution uses execution feedback and retains candidates that work on different examples. | **[Beyond Prompts: Measuring and Optimizing LLM Tool-Agent Harnesses](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05736v2)** — September 9 revision | PRISM compares prompt/tool-boundary optimization with held-out reliability and search-budget measures on BFCL and two tau domains. Results use scorecard subsets and constrained tool-boundary edit types; not a full public leaderboard or financial-research result. Adopt generic interface correctness where warranted; do not add semantic keyword guards or accumulate case-specific middleware. |

## Retrieval, context and memory

| Work / inspected version | Relevant finding and transfer limit |
| --- | --- |
| **[Rethinking Reasoning-Intensive Retrieval / BRIGHT-Pro](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.04018v1)** — May 5, 2026 | Evaluates complementary reasoning aspects in both static retrieval and fixed/adaptive agent loops. BGE-Reasoner-8B leads the paper's fixed-round agentic table; the proposed RTriever-4B is **not** its universal winner. Compare aspect coverage at fixed depth and downstream quality, rather than swapping embeddings because a new paper claims improvement. The domains and human labels differ from finance. |
| **[RECON](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.10448v2)** — June 6 revision | A learned, frozen observation compressor inside an RL-trained search loop; evaluates small search agents on QA. Direct precedent for compression within an investigation, rather than only prebuilt document summaries. Its exact-match metric does not measure complete evidence faithfulness; the paper explicitly identifies that limit. Transfer to our API-based Pro/Flash loop needs a separate comparison including compressor cost. |
| **[ConCise](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.28361v1)** — June 13 | Append-only structured conclusions and fused reasoning/conclusion generation reduce repeated state payloads without a separately trained compressor. Reported token savings accompany mixed accuracy changes, including losses in several configurations. Useful low-complexity comparison, not demonstrated lossless compression or equivalent cost savings for our workload. Preserving an extracted conclusion does not ensure that extraction was correct or that omitted evidence was unimportant. |
| **[Agentic Context Engineering (ACE)](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04618v3)** — March 29, 2026 revision | Incrementally maintained playbooks address loss from repeated summary rewriting; tests include agent and finance tasks. Useful precedent for learning reusable guidance instead of endlessly adding global warnings. Reflector errors, context size, adaptation cost and stale/misapplied guidance still need held-out assessment. Its publication-time benchmark claims are not current universal SOTA. |
| **[Recursive Language Models](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24601v3)** — May 11 revision | Treats long input as an inspectable external object, allowing programmatic reads and model calls over selected parts. A plausible alternative to forcing every task through a lossy summary. Results depend on workload and execution policy; our small Flash hierarchy experiment did not implement or evaluate this system. Include complete call/latency tails, not just input compression. |
| **[Think, But Don't Overthink: Reproducing RLMs](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.02615v1)** — March 3 | Independent, small reproduction with only 20 examples per condition reports model/task-dependent benefit and deterioration with deeper recursion. This is a useful counterexample, not a decisive general refutation. It motivates shallow/adaptive context access as a baseline and explicit overhead measurement. |
| **[MemoryArena](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16313v2)** — September 17 revision; ICML 2026 per arXiv | Human-crafted, interdependent multi-session tasks evaluate whether memory improves later action, not only conversation recall. Strongly relevant to Zopedia's longitudinal objective. Benchmark/session structure differs from financial investigation; memory retrieval and serving cost must remain part of our comparison. |
| **[AMA-Bench / AMA-Agent](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.22769v4)** — May 27 revision | Trajectory-based memory, LLM-extracted state/dependency graphs and selective graph/programmatic retrieval; includes downstream environment comparisons beyond QA. A useful candidate against simple provenance memory. Its state-transition “causality” does **not** identify causal business/market relationships. Extra extraction, storage and retrieval must earn a net benefit. |

Our retained-chunk path already combines independent full-text and embedding candidates with reciprocal rank fusion and source diversification: [storage](../../../services/saa/storage.py), [semantic index](../../../services/saa/semantic_index.py). The document-search path and other prepared-evidence paths have their own ranking/limits; do not assume all consumers have identical recall. PostgreSQL full-text ranking is not BM25. The current stack is a legitimate baseline, not evidence of SOTA retrieval or a reason to rewrite solely to obtain a fashionable ranker.

Two close foundational precedents prevent overstating novelty: **[Sufficient Context](https://research.google/pubs/sufficient-context-a-new-lens-on-retrieval-augmented-generation-systems-2/)** (ICLR 2025) separates insufficient evidence from failure to use sufficient evidence; **[Lost in the Middle](https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.9/)** (TACL 2024) shows that evidence position affects its use. Our open question is narrower: when a derived interpretation is wrong, what causes original evidence and later review to repair or preserve it? Merely observing that “available evidence was ignored” is already established.

## Cost interpretation of the most relevant new proposal

Using HSAD's reported finance numbers, not our workload:

```text
Quality:       52.9% -> 56.6% = +3.7 percentage points
Normal cost:  $0.93 -> $1.14  = +22.6%
High-effort comparison: $1.81 -> $1.14 = -37.0%
```

Thus broad adoption would not meet our +20% requirement without offsets or selective use. The study has training/strategy-collection costs too. Reported serving efficiency does not pay those automatically. Our experiment should compare no extra guidance, improved existing prompts, retrieved examples and selective guidance at the **same workload settings**, then measure complete cost. The paper's absolute dollars and gains are not projections for Pro/Flash in our engine.

## A SOTA-informed design for this system

Keep the [clean responsibility flow](README.md#research-objective). Make sources and memory versioned and inspectable; let the analyst use search, originals, tables and Python within the existing settings; preserve useful evidence/results across continuation; have review check material claims independently and let the analyst resolve disagreements.

Around that flow, build an **offline improvement loop**: reviewed decision data → independent objective/judgment/outcome labels → controlled alternatives → prompt/guidance/allocation candidates → untouched evaluation at matched coverage and complete cost. Start with better use of current stages. Add learned selection or richer context/memory only when the experiment demonstrates a gain. Avoid a separate production evaluator call merely because the offline evaluation needs an independent judge.

Use deterministic machinery for source identity, timestamps, arithmetic execution, payload integrity, replay and accounting. Keep semantic interpretation, relevance, investigation choice and hypothesis generation with models and reviewed learning signals. Do not convert qualitative failure examples into branches or build an elaborate defensive policy engine.

For longitudinal discovery, measure what future work actually improves: verified new leads, earlier discovery, better follow-up choices and, where relevant, calibrated forecasts or executable decisions. That remains a high-ROI research opportunity even if the basic orchestration matches current practice. The [prioritized research questions](README.md#highest-roi-research-questions) specify the next comparisons rather than claiming novelty from architecture alone.
