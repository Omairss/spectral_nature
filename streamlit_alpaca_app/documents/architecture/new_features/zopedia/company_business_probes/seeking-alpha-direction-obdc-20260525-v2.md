Story:

Blue Owl Capital Corporation (OBDC) is a publicly traded business development company (BDC) that originates and manages a diversified portfolio of direct loans to U.S. middle-market companies. Its primary revenue sources are interest income from senior secured, subordinated, and mezzanine loans, along with fees and equity kickers. The company is externally managed by Blue Owl Credit Advisors, part of the larger Blue Owl Capital platform with $159 billion in credit AUM. Recent financial results (Q4 2025) show stable net investment income of $0.36 per share (adjusted), a 10% annualized dividend yield, and a NAV of $14.81, slightly down due to credit markdowns on a few names. A significant positive event was the sale of $1.4 billion in loans at 99.7% of par to institutional investors, with demand exceeding supply, validating portfolio quality. OBDC maintains strong liquidity of $4.1 billion and a conservative use target (0.9x–1.25x debt/equity), with a new $300 million buyback authorized. Risks include credit performance (2.3% of loans on non-accrual at cost) and management of the non-traded OBDC II fund, though the asset sale addresses that. Overall, OBDC’s business model is confirmed with strong investor demand and stable earnings, but execution risks remain around credit quality and fund management.
