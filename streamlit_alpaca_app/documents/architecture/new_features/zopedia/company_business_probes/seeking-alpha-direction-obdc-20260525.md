Story:

Blue Owl Capital Corporation (OBDC) is a publicly traded business development company (BDC) that originates and manages a portfolio of primarily senior secured loans to U.S. middle-market companies. It generates income from interest and fees on its loan portfolio, and is externally managed by Blue Owl Credit Advisors, part of the larger Blue Owl Capital platform with over $150 billion in credit AUM. As of Q4 2025, OBDC's portfolio had a fair value of $15.3 billion across 230 companies, with 78% senior secured and 96% floating rate, providing natural rate sensitivity. Net investment income has been stable around $0.36-$0.38 per share, supporting a quarterly dividend of $0.37 (yield ~10%). The company saw strong institutional demand for a $1.4 billion asset sale in early 2026, confirming asset quality. However, non-accruals have ticked up slightly (1.1% fair value in Q4 2025) due to credit markdowns on a few names, and the OBDC II vehicle faced structural criticism, though management responded with enhanced liquidity. Overall, OBDC's business model is confirmed with stable earnings and strong demand, but credit quality and execution on the OBDC II structure remain monitored risks.
