## Objective and correction

The system maximizes useful relevance with high recall. It accepts noisy leads,
indirect relationships and reasonable hypotheses. It is not a source-proof or
causal-certification system. The user explicitly corrected an overly restrictive
direction during this repair. Review improves explanations and follow-up; it does
not decide whether a valid research artifact is allowed to exist. Missing evidence
is a research opportunity, not an automatic exclusion.

Removed the newly introduced blanket shared review gate and Home-wide acceptance
boolean. Replaced proof-only editorial instructions and case-specific accounting
rules with discovery, relevance and coherent explanation. Removed older writer
phrase checks that erased generated explanations, tool-count confidence caps,
and the fixed 4% unresolved-move admission cutoff. Source-event intake now retains
older supplied reports for the LLM to assess in dated context instead of applying
a separate three-day exclusion. Available researched bundles
reach editorial ranking; display limits apply after that ranking. LLMs assess
meaning and relevance. Code protects execution budgets, schemas and permissions.
Durable knowledge writes retain their separate authorized, audited policy.

The first dev Home execution also exposed an implicit admission boundary: a
sixty-second news nomination timed out while reading fifty reports plus the full
market universe. Source-led nomination now reads reports independently and has a
configurable budget. More importantly, final synthesis receives the full retained
source intake and can create stories outside the researched candidate list.
Focused nomination allocates acquisition effort; it does not bound publication.
The same canonical materializer handles edited and newly discovered stories, with
LLM ranking before display limits. No additional model or verifier is introduced.
