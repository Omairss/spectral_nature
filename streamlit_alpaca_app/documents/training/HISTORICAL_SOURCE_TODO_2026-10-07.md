## Storage estimate

The existing account is Central US, Standard LRS, Hot. Source objects and cleaned Parquet are compressed and deduplicated by content hash. At an illustrative **$0.02/GB-month**, 100 GB is about **$2/month** and 1 TB about **$20/month**, before request operations, egress and processing. This is a planning estimate, not the account's contracted rate. Full tick data, world-news bodies and large social dumps can exceed that volume by orders of magnitude; estimate their bytes from source catalogs before fan-out. [Azure Blob pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/storage/blobs/).

## Research priority

Prioritize missing coverage by its ability to improve out-of-period return/downside prediction and the research decisions it directs. Preserve contradictory explanations and ordinary background periods. Price movement is a supervision signal for where to investigate; it does not automatically prove an explanation or make an unperformed investigation a failed research example.
